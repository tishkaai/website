Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

The short version

You can cancel any time — no lock-in, no minimum term. Give 30 days notice. If you leave mid-month, we refund the unused part of that month's fee. The £500 setup fee is not refundable, because it pays for work that's already delivered.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time. There's no lock-in and no minimum term — the same promise we make on our pricing page. To cancel, email pete@tishka.ai and give 30 days notice.

Monthly fees

The monthly fee covers the month you've paid for. If you've paid for a full month but leave in the middle of it, we refund the unused balance, pro-rata. If you leave at the end of a month, no refund is needed — the month was delivered in full.

Setup fee

The £500 setup fee is not refundable. It pays for the initial build — your second brain, connected to your tools, plus your first workflow — which is delivered before the monthly service begins.

Other payments

Extra workflows are quoted per job at a fixed price, agreed with you before any work starts. If you believe a refund is due in any other situation, email pete@tishka.ai and we'll sort it out.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or to request a refund, contact us at pete@tishka.ai.
