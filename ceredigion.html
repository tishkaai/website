Tishka AI is run from Cardigan. The job is one thing: build a business brain for a small business, one clean home for everything the company knows, filled from the apps it already uses, its documents, and a short interview with the owner. Then the repetitive admin goes to AI, and a person approves what goes out.

We work with businesses across Ceredigion. Cardigan and the Teifi valley are on the doorstep. Aberystwyth sits up the coast, Aberaeron down the estuary road, Lampeter in the middle of the county. The work itself happens on calls and shared screens, so a business in Aberystwyth gets the same service as one in Cardigan town.

What a business in Ceredigion can hand to AI

The repeating work is much the same anywhere in the county. Emails that need sorting. Invoices that need chasing. Records that need keeping. Questions that get answered twice a week by whoever is nearest.

What changes is the mix around that work. A holiday let spends the season answering the same guest questions. A farm keeps records, forms and supplier chasing moving. A builder chases quotes and payments. A small practice keeps deadlines and client records straight. Each of those is a job worth scoring before anything is automated: how many hours it takes, whether it repeats, and what happens if it goes wrong once.

Where the score is good, AI drafts and a named person approves. Nothing sends unchecked. Financial, compliance and legal work stays approval-only, always.
