FAQ

Questions you might have.

Why no tiers?

Most businesses we work with need the same core things: a second brain, an agent that knows their business, and the admin off their plate. We keep it simple.

What if I need more than the included support?

Additional workflows are quoted per job at a fixed price. You'll always know the cost upfront.

Can I add voice AI later?

Yes. It works best once your second brain is built — the voice agent draws from the same knowledge base.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. The only additional cost is your AI model usage (paid directly to the provider, typically £15–30/month) and any third-party software subscriptions you choose to keep.

What if I want to switch to a different AI tool later?

Your second brain is just files. Any AI agent that can read files can use it. You're not locked into us or our software.
