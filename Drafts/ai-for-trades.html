    Where the week actually goes.

    18% of payments arrive late

    Invoice chasing is quiet, unpaid work

    Construction has the worst late-payment record of the major sectors — 18% of payments arrive past terms. Small firms are hit hardest: 49% say customers pay later than agreed. And 33% won't chase formally to protect the relationship — so the polite chases fall to you, by hand. DBT, 19 Sept 2024.

    20% — or 30% if you're not registered

    CIS deductions eat the margin twice

    Under CIS, contractors deduct 20% from a registered subcontractor's payments — 30% if unregistered — and the verification, monthly returns and statements run all year. The matching and chasing around them is the drain. GOV.UK CIS, checked 2026-09-03.

    24% of late payments = admin errors

    The paperwork that never gets typed up

    Government research attributes 24% of late payments to administrative errors — failing to log invoices among them. In the trades: the write-up done from memory days later, the material order never recorded, the cert in a van folder. Every one a small future chase. DBT, 19 Sept 2024.

    Questions trade owners actually ask.

    What does it cost, honestly?
    Packages start at £300/month plus a one-off setup — £1,500 for Front Desk, £3,000 for Ops Desk, £5,000 for AI Ops Partner. Setup is split 50/50 (half on agreement, half on go-live). No lock-in: 30 days' notice cancels the monthly. The first diagnostic is free.
    My customers ring — is a chatbot going to answer them?
    No chatbot answers your customers. The system listens to the inbox, texts and voicemails, files what it hears into a job, and drafts a reply in your words. You read it and approve — the customer hears from you, not from a robot. If a job needs a human decision, it goes to your morning list, not out the door.
    Will it pester customers who owe me money?
    It chases the way you'd chase on a good day: logged steps, polite reminders, sensible gaps — and it stops for a human decision before anything gets pointed. Government research says a third of businesses won't chase at all to protect the relationship; a calm, consistent ladder is the middle path.
    What if it files a job or an invoice wrong?
    It drafts; you approve — a wrong field is a two-second fix, not a sent invoice. Missing details stay blank rather than invented. And every write-up comes from a voice note or the original message, so the record trails back to what was actually said.
    What do you need from us to build it?
    Read access to the inbox (or a forwarding rule), your job list or spreadsheet, and ten minutes to walk us through how a job flows today. No new logins for the lads, nothing for customers to install, and your data stays yours: exports and documentation come with the build.
