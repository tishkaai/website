rules/pricing.md
The rules this design keeps. The morning brief stays one block, max 6 lines. The standup is written from records, blockers first, walking the five lanes, one line per item. Leads never sends, it has no send capability. Every lane names who taps. Nothing sends until someone taps. Lanes are the five defaults; onboarding may rename or add in the client's own words.
