    £50k → £30k → £20k

    The MTD wave, phased and dated

    MTD for Income Tax went mandatory on 6 April 2026 for income over £50,000 — over £30,000 follows from 6 April 2027, over £20,000 from 6 April 2028 (GOV.UK). From September 2026, HMRC force-signs-up anyone who hasn't signed up. GOV.UK MTD guidance, checked 2026-09-03.

    18 Nov 2025 — already law

    Companies House verification is inside the workload too

    Identity verification became a legal requirement on 18 November 2025, in a 12-month transition: directors' codes ride on each confirmation statement, PSCs have 14-day windows — and firms will need to register as authorised agents (ACSPs) to file for clients. Your own compliance layer, on top of every client's. GOV.UK Companies House guidance, checked 2026-09-03.
