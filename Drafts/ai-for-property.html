    Questions agency owners actually ask.

    What does it cost, honestly?
    Packages start at £300/month plus a one-off setup — £1,500 for Front Desk, £3,000 for Ops Desk, £5,000 for AI Ops Partner. Setup is split 50/50 (half on agreement, half on go-live). No lock-in: 30 days' notice cancels the monthly. The first diagnostic is free.
    Our CRM already has AI — why would we need this too?
    Because CRM AI lives inside the CRM, and most of it is built for the sales side — enquiries, instructions, descriptions. The compliance calendar, contractor chases, arrears steps and landlord updates sit between your CRM, your inbox and your spreadsheets. We build the glue across them — and we'll say so when your existing tool already covers something.
    Will it email tenants and landlords without a human checking?
    No. Everything starts in draft and waits for approval. Routine categories — a booking confirmation, a standard chase — can switch to auto later, per category, your choice. Compliance notices, arrears steps and money messages stay approval-only permanently.
    What if it misses a compliance date?
    The watchlist is built from your own cert records, and every date is visible — nothing hides inside the system. The 60/30/7-day nudges mean a date is flagged three times before it arrives, and anything unusual escalates to a human task rather than an automated send. That said, the system assists your compliance process; final accountability stays with the agency, as it does today.
    What do you need from us to build it?
    Read access to the inbox (or a forwarding rule), your compliance date list — even a spreadsheet — and a look at whatever tracks jobs today. No new logins for staff, nothing for tenants or landlords to install, and your data stays yours: exports and documentation come with the build.
