    Questions recruitment owners actually ask.

    What does it cost, honestly?
    Packages start at £300/month plus a one-off setup — £1,500 for Front Desk, £3,000 for Ops Desk, £5,000 for AI Ops Partner. Setup is split 50/50 (half on agreement, half on go-live). No lock-in: 30 days' notice cancels the monthly. The first diagnostic is free.
    We already pay for an ATS — why would we need this too?
    Because the ATS stops at its own walls. The formatting, the feedback chasing, the timesheets, the inbox triage — the 2024 Loxo and Firefish reviews show branded submissions and formatting are exactly where users still work around their system. We build the glue across whatever you run, and we'll tell you when your existing tool already covers something.
    Will it email clients and candidates without a human checking?
    No. Everything starts in draft and waits for approval. Once you trust a routine category — a standard chase, a scheduling nudge — you can switch it to auto, per category, your choice. Financial, compliance and anything client-facing sensitive stays approval-only permanently.
    What happens when it gets a CV or a name wrong?
    It drafts; you approve — so a wrong field is a two-second fix, not a sent email. Missing fields stay blank rather than being invented. We never claim 100% accuracy; we claim AI drafts in ~2 minutes what took 30 by hand, with a human still reading it.
    What do you need from us to build it?
    Read access to the inbox (or a forwarding rule), your CV template, and a look at whatever tracks candidates today — even if that's a spreadsheet. No new logins for your consultants, nothing for candidates or clients to install, and your data stays yours: exports and documentation come with the build.
