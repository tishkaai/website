FAQs

What does it cost?
The diagnostic: £499, one fixed fee. A one-hour discovery call, the ranked report, and a follow-up call to walk it through. Every pound credits against any build we do together. Builds after that are scoped from your numbers and quoted before anything starts.
What if you don't find 5 hours?
Then the diagnostic is free. The £499 comes back in full, no conversation needed. It's the same standard we hold everywhere: we answer for the work.
Does the AI send things without asking?
No. Everything starts in draft mode. Replies queue for a human to approve. Once you're confident, you can switch routine categories to auto-send, per category, any time. Financial, compliance and legal always stay approval-only.
Do we have to change our software?
No. We connect the tools already in use (inbox, job system, CRM, accounting) and file everything where it belongs. No new logins for your team or your customers.
Does the team have to touch the tech?
No. We build it, set it up, and run it. The team just gets the results.
Will this replace my team?
No. We take on the repetitive work your team does despite being hired for something harder. The bottleneck is rarely too few people. It's people doing the wrong work.
We already use Notion or AI tools. Why do we need this?
Notion is where you write down what you know. A Company Brain knows the business, watches it live, and warns you before things slip. We keep it running. The tools are plumbing. We're the brain and the person who keeps it sharp.
What if it doesn't work for our business?
Start with one automation and judge it in weeks, not months. Nothing here is sold as magic: AI drafts, humans approve, and the numbers come from your own systems, not our slides.
