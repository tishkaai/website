Is it safe to let AI email my customers?

Everything starts as a draft. The AI writes the reply, and it queues for a person to approve before anything sends. Nothing goes out that a person hasn't seen.

Financial, compliance and legal work always stays approval-only. You can switch routine categories to auto-send later, per category, any time. The check is what makes the hand-over safe, not a tax on it.

Builds are scoped from your numbers and quoted before anything starts. After that, a retainer keeps it running and accurate, month after month.

Can I do this myself with Zapier or ChatGPT, or do I need a partner?

Honest answer: DIY genuinely works for simple repeating jobs. If the job is one step and never changes, do it yourself.

Where a partner earns their keep is wiring the tools together, keeping the knowledge accurate, and the person who maintains it. If the job touches several tools and needs to stay right, that's where we come in.

Will AI replace my admin team?

No. It takes the repetitive work that stops your team doing what you hired them for. The bottleneck is rarely too few people. It's people doing the wrong work.

Your team keeps the judgement. AI takes the retyping.

Does my data stay mine?

Yes. Four promises cover it.

Your data is yours. We hold it so the automation can run; you own it.

We see status, not content. On our side a job shows as ran, failed, or waiting for your approval — not what was in it. We don't read your emails, client records, or invoices to run the service.

You can export or delete everything, any time. Your brain is plain markdown files in a private repo plus your workflows. No proprietary format. Ask and we hand you a full export and delete our copies. No lock-in.

GDPR compliant, confidential, no lock-in. UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018. Your data never trains AI models, so it can't be used against you.

What is a business brain?

A business brain is one clean home for everything your business knows. It holds your clients, jobs, deadlines, preferences and history. It's filled from the apps you already use, your documents, and a short interview. Plain files you own. No lock-in. Any AI tool can read it.

What is approval-first AI?

Approval-first AI means everything starts as a draft. The AI drafts the reply, and it queues for a person to approve. Nothing sends until a person taps. Routine categories can switch to auto-send later, per category. Financial, compliance and legal always stay approval-only.
